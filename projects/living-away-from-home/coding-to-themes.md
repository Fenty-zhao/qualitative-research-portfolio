### What is original, and what was reconstructed?

- **Surviving original materials:** Chinese interview transcript with speaker turns/timestamps; a Chinese working draft listing codes under Theme 1; a later English draft; final submitted report.
- **Retrospective editorial work:** the rows above linking selected transcript passages to the saved codes and final themes; concise English translations and descriptions.
- **Not claimed:** comprehensive transcript coding, intercoder reliability, saturation, NVivo/ATLAS.ti work, or an original timestamped coding spreadsheet.
- **AI support:** AI contributed to organising and articulating codes/themes and drafting research text. The interviewer also manually reviewed and interpreted the transcript; the research was not conducted using dedicated qualitative analysis software.

## An example of an interview insight challenging the researcher

The interviewer explored whether the participant's attitude could be understood through a broad account of East Asian culture. The participant questioned that framing instead of endorsing it. This is valuable **counter-evidence against researcher-imposed assumptions**, and informed the later reflection on interview influence.

## Responsible use of extracts

This page uses short translated or paraphrased segments. It intentionally excludes names of people mentioned, precise personal background details, unredacted transcript sections, and any files from which the participant might be easily identified. The participant authorised anonymised presentation, **not** publication of the full raw transcript.
